DeckManager.random_deck_maker() — draft sequence
===================================================

This describes what random_deck_maker() actually does, in order, as a
reference for discussing/tuning its behavior. Each step is labeled to make
it easy to refer to ("phase 6", "the Protector fill", etc.) when
requesting changes.

Card pool restriction
------------------------
The legal pool (both for the hero and for every ally below) is restricted
to FULLY IMPLEMENTED cards only: data_status == "verified" AND
engine_status == "" (empty). An unimplemented hero's power, or an
unimplemented ally's text, would just be dead/unusable content on the
card, so neither is ever offered. This restriction can shrink a faction's
legal pool below what's needed to fill a deck (see "Known limitations"
below) — it's checked here in the manual because it constrains every
phase that follows, not just hero selection.

Phase 1 — Pick a random hero
-------------------------------
Picks uniformly at random among fully-implemented heroes. The hero's
alignment (Alliance/Horde) and class drive every phase below.

Phase 2 — Build the legal ally pool
--------------------------------------
Allies only (abilities aren't played by anyone yet, so they're excluded
from the pool entirely — see DeckManager.gd for the disabled-but-correct
logic to re-enable once ability effects exist). A card is legal if:
  - it's fully implemented (see above), AND
  - its alignment isn't the hero's opposing faction (neutral/blank
    alignment is always allowed), AND
  - if it's a Pet (subtype contains "Pet"), its class matches the hero's
    class (or it's classless) — Pets are class-restricted regardless of
    faction.

Phase 3 — Hero-specific synergy picks
----------------------------------------------
Certain heroes get guaranteed allies that synergise with or thematically
reflect their hero power. Only heroes with an explicit entry are affected;
all others skip this phase silently:
  - Boris Brightbeard  → 2x Crazy Igvand (fellow Dwarf Protector — tanks
                          in front of the healer hero; flavor + defense)
  - Omedus the Punisher → 4x Mias the Putrid (forces opponent discards,
                           directly synergises with Omedus's hero power
                           that scales off empty hands)
  - Ta'zo               → 2x Vesh'ral (same class/faction/damage type,
                           Ferocity lets him attack immediately — fits
                           Ta'zo's aggressive Fire Mage identity)

Phase 4 — Faction-defining identity core
----------------------------------------------
Every random deck gets a fixed set of named cards that express the
faction's playstyle identity, regardless of what else ends up in the deck:
  - Alliance hero → 4x Parvink (Protector core)
                    2x Nerra Lifeboon (+1 health aura, keeps allies alive;
                     fits Alliance's survivability identity)
  - Horde hero    → 1x Guardian Steelhorn, 2x Ka'tali Stonetusk,
                     1x Kulan Earthguard (Protector core)
                    2x Zorm Stonefury (+1 ATK while attacking; rewards
                     aggressive play and synergises with Ferocity allies,
                     fitting Horde's offensive identity)
(If a hero is ever neither Alliance nor Horde, this phase does nothing —
not currently possible.)

Phase 5 — Class-specific defensive Pet
----------------------------------------------
If the hero's class is Warlock: 2x Sarmoth (a Pet — legal for any Warlock
hero of either faction, since its alignment is neutral). Sarmoth restricts
opposing attackers to itself once in play, stacking another layer of
defense on top of whatever Protectors landed in phases 3–4.

Phase 6 — Generic Protector fill
---------------------------------------
4 additional Protector copies, picked one at a time at random from
whichever legal Protectors still have room under the 4-copy cap. Runs
AFTER all specific named phases (3–5) so it never pre-empts a targeted
pick — its role is to fill the remaining generic Protector requirement
with whatever the pool still has available. This can mean 4 copies of one
card, or a spread across several. If the legal Protector pool is fully
capped out (not currently possible), it just adds fewer than 4 and moves on.

Phase 7 — Guarantee 12 cards per cheap tier
------------------------------------------------
For each of cost 1, 2, and 3 (checked in that order): shuffle the legal
allies at that cost, and add copies (respecting the 4-copy cap, and
whatever copies phases 3–6 already used) until that tier has at least
12 cards or the tier's pool is exhausted.

Phase 8 — Fill to 60
-------------------------
Shuffle the ENTIRE legal pool and add copies (respecting the 4-copy cap)
until the deck reaches 60 cards or the pool is exhausted.


Known limitations
--------------------
- A faction needs enough total fully-implemented legal allies (at 4 copies
  each) to reach 60, or the deck comes up short — phases 7/8 simply run out
  of legal cards to add and stop. Check current counts before assuming a
  faction can field a full deck.
- Phases 3–6 use _add_card_copies() which merges into existing entries;
  phases 7/8 append new entries for the same id rather than merging. This
  is harmless (Deck.to_draw_pile() flattens every entry regardless), just
  not the tidiest data shape if inspecting a generated deck directly.
- Phase 5 only checks for "Warlock" by name — if a second class ever gets
  its own guaranteed pick, this phase will need to become a lookup table
  instead of an if-chain.
